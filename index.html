blue41studio

An independent studio run by one developer who loves sport and games. blue41 started as a passion project and keeps growing one app at a time: tools for coaches and players, for the modding scene, and a few games along the way.

Our apps

blue41 Playbooks

Android

Your playbook in your pocket.

A play designer and playbook maker for flag 5v5 and tackle 11v11 football coaches, players and teams: draw and animate plays, organise your schemes, export play sheets and wrist-coach cards. Free to start with one-time upgrades — no subscription, no ads, no account. 8 languages.

blue41 PES2021 Mod Studio

Windows Beta · v0.2.0

PES 2021 modding, without the headaches.

The all-in-one tool for eFootball PES 2021 and its patches: Sider settings and modules made easy, your own crowd chants for any team, Become a Legend helpers — with a backup and an undo for every change. Free and ad-free.
